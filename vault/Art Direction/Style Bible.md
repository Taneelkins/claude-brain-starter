# 📖 Style Bible

> One section per game. Every UI, VFX and 3D task reads its game's section first. Back to [[Art Direction]]

## <Game name>
- **In one line:** e.g. "cosy low-poly winter, chunky silhouettes, warm lights in cold blue".
- **Palette:** 5–8 hex colours with jobs (background, accent, danger…).
- **Shapes:** chunky / sharp / round; how much detail; what to avoid.
- **Materials:** flat colour / PBR / stylised; roughness range.
- **3D build method:** box-built in Blender / AI-generated + Substance / Roblox parts.
- **UI language:** fonts, corner radius, borders, how things animate.
- **References:** 3–5 images or games, and what to take from each.
